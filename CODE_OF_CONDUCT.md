# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. This document is short on purpose: it is not a legal
instrument, it is a description of how we intend to treat each other.

## The standard

Behave as though you had to sit across from the person you are replying to.

That covers most of it. Concretely, in issues, pull requests, commit messages and
any other space belonging to this project:

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith, including when
  they are wrong. Most bad patches come from a misunderstanding, not from
  carelessness.
- Criticise the code, not the person. "This breaks pairing on a second device"
  is useful; "did you even test this" is not, and says nothing the first sentence
  did not.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer. A change can be good and still not belong
  in this project. Maintainers owe you a reason, not a merge.
- Take no for an answer once it is given, and do not reopen the same argument in
  a new thread.

## Not acceptable

Harassment of any kind. Insults, and demeaning or sustained personal remarks.
Attacks or slurs based on who someone is: gender, gender identity, sexual
orientation, disability, appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, age,
religion, or anything else of that nature. Sexual attention or advances.
Publishing someone's private information. Deliberately derailing discussions.

None of this becomes acceptable because it is framed as a joke, or because the
technical point underneath happened to be correct.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project owns, and whenever someone is
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to `alexis.raitano@myffu.fr`. Reports are read by the
maintainer, kept confidential, and answered.

You do not need to be certain, and you do not need to have been the target. If
something felt wrong to you, that is enough of a reason to write.

## What happens next

The maintainer decides, and says what was decided. Depending on what happened,
the response ranges from a private word, to a removed comment, to a temporary or
permanent block from the project's spaces. Repeated or severe behaviour goes
straight to the far end of that range.

Enforcement here is one person's judgement. That is the honest description of a
project this size, and it is better said out loud than dressed up as a committee.
